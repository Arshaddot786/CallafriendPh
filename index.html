Why it matters

Loneliness is a quiet crisis, and talking helps.

Many adults go days without a real conversation. Busy schedules, moving to a new city, working from home, or losing touch with friends can leave people feeling isolated. In 2023 the U.S. Surgeon General warned of a loneliness and isolation epidemic and called social connection a public health priority.

A regular, friendly face on the screen can change how a day feels. You do not need to be in crisis to want company. Sometimes you just want someone to listen, laugh with you and ask how your week went.

A good conversation is one of the simplest ways to feel less alone.

A lighter mood

Warm, face-to-face conversation can ease stress and help a heavy day feel more manageable.

Feeling heard

Having someone give you their full attention builds a sense of belonging and self-worth.

Healthier routines

A regular call gives the week a rhythm, and many people find it easier to stay active and engaged.

Confidence and culture

Chat in relaxed English, share stories and learn about life in the Philippines.

CallAFriendPH offers friendly company. It is not therapy, counselling or medical care. If you are struggling, please reach out to a qualified professional or a local crisis line.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it video, audio or text?

Sessions start on video by default, so you can see and talk with your companion face to face. If you prefer, choose audio only or text chat when you book, or switch during the session. The price is the same.

Is this a dating service?

No. CallAFriendPH is for friendly, platonic conversation only. Romantic or sexual requests lead to removal from the platform.

What can we talk about?

Almost anything friendly: your day, hobbies, travel, food, music, practicing English or just relaxing. Companions are not therapists, so they will not give medical or legal advice.

How do plans work?

Weekly sessions must be used within 7 days and monthly sessions within 30 days. Plans renew automatically and you can cancel at any time.

Can I choose the same companion every time?

Yes. Many people book the same companion regularly, and you can try different ones until you find a good fit.

What do I need to join a session?

A phone or laptop and a stable internet connection. For video you also need a camera and microphone. Text chat needs neither. Everything runs in your browser.
