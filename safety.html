Safety and community

Friendly, respectful and safe for everyone.

CallAFriendPH is for friendly conversation between adults. These guidelines protect customers and companions.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Draft for legal review. A lawyer should review this text before launch.

Customers

  • Be kind and respectful. Companions are people, not services to be tested.
  • Keep conversations friendly and platonic.
  • Do not ask for contact details, in-person meetings, money or gifts.
  • Do not record or screenshot a session without consent.
  • You can end a session at any time.

Companions

  • Be warm, honest and professional.
  • Keep every conversation on the platform. Never share personal contact details.
  • Never accept or ask for money, gifts or loans.
  • End any session that feels uncomfortable and report it. You will not be penalised.
  • Do not give medical, legal or financial advice.

Scam awareness

A companion will never ask you for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, bank transfers or personal contact details. If anyone does, end the conversation and report it. Be careful with anyone who asks you to move to another app.

How to report a concern

Use the report option during or after a session, or contact us through the Contact page and choose "Safety concern". Reports are reviewed by our team. Serious reports lead to immediate suspension while we look into them. We may warn, suspend or permanently remove accounts that break the rules.

If you or someone else needs urgent help

CallAFriendPH is friendly company, not crisis support or emergency care. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the United States, Canada and the Philippines, 999 in the United Kingdom, 000 in Australia).

United States and CanadaCall or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.
United KingdomCall Samaritans free on 116 123.
AustraliaCall Lifeline on 13 11 14.
